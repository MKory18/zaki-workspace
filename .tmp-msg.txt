feat(profile): the face travels with the name on every list

A person's picture was drawn only in the chat and the people dock; every other
list still drew their initials. The address now rides along with the name:

- the staff directory (/members, /teams) and a colleague's profile page;
- the picker of «رسالة جديدة» and the two topic pickers;
- a department's members, in the cards and in its sheet;
- a call: the people in the room, whoever started it, and the ringing card.

A GUEST keeps names only: their lists carry no picture address at all, which
the bytes route would refuse anyway.

tests/db/avatar-surfaces.test.ts pins each surface (six cases, all red before
this change), and tests/db/accounts-list.test.ts now expects avatarUrl on the
roster contract.
