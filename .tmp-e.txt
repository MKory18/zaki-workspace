fix(phase-e): two writes that left no trail now leave one

The code map (§9) named the two route handlers that write to the database and leave
nothing behind: `/api/boards/[id]` (a board renamed, archived, or its columns reordered)
and `/api/tasks/[id]/comments/[commentId]` (a comment edited or deleted). Every other write
of the same kind already wrote an `AuditLog` or a `TaskActivity` row, so "who renamed this
board", "who moved this column" and "where did my comment go" had no answer.

- `board.updated` and `board.columns_reordered` on the board routes, with the actor, the
  company, the board and what changed.
- `task.comment_edited` / `task.comment_deleted` on the task's own feed, with the comment id.

`tests/db/phase-e-trail.test.ts` (4): each of the four writes leaves its row, and editing
somebody else's comment still answers 403 and writes nothing. Three of the four fail on the
branch before the fix.
